Governance

Who is responsible, and how decisions are made

Waqt is a standard first and software second. Its rules are written down, versioned, and changed through proposals and decision records. Waqt welcomes qualified experts and contributors.

How the specification changes

  1. ProposeAnyone may open a change proposal: the question, evidence, proposed text and vectors.
  2. EvidencePromotions need measurements from two independent implementations — or a stated reason why only one exists.
  3. ReviewAt least one reviewer who is not the author; proposals stay open at least seven days.
  4. DecideA decision record is written. Dissent is recorded in the dissenter’s words. A record is accepted only when a named maintainer ratifies it.
  5. ChangeRequirement, vectors and prose change together, with a changelog entry.

Principles

  • Written requirements govern; test vectors illustrate.
  • Facts are normative; conventions are configurable — astronomy is fixed, calendar providers and prayer-method angles are choices you pass in.
  • A clean report is not a goal — open questions stay open until genuinely settled.
  • Waqt provides temporal facts, not religious rulings.
