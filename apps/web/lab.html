Experience Lab

Waqt, experienced — not just explained

Five short demonstrations. Each is driven by the real Waqt API; if it cannot be reached they switch to a frozen, labelled sample. Nothing here calculates Waqt in your browser.

01

The Waqt Clock — drag through the day

Move the slider from one Maghrib to the next; markers are the events the API returned for this Waqt day.

SCRUB PREVIEW The slider picks a position on the day; the reading shown is that position’s elapsed time since Maghrib, not a live reading.
