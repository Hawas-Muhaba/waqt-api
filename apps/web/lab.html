Experience Lab

Waqt, experienced — not just explained

Five short demonstrations. Each one is driven by the real Waqt API; if the API cannot be reached, they switch to a frozen, clearly labelled sample. Nothing here calculates Waqt in your browser.

01 The Waqt Clock02 Maghrib Reset03 Next Event04 Waqt vs Midnight05 Build an Application
01

The Waqt Clock — drag through the day

Move the slider from one Maghrib to the next. The markers are the events the API returned for this Waqt day.

SCRUB PREVIEW The slider chooses a position on the day; the reading shown is that position’s elapsed time since Maghrib, not a live reading.

02

Maghrib Reset — move the place and the date

Pick a place and a date; the API tells you where that day’s Maghrib boundary falls. Watch it move.
