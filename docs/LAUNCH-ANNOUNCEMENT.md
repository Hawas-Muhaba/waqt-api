> 🌍 **What is Waqt?**  
> Rather than treating prayer times as isolated alarm notifications, Waqt models the day as a continuous, mathematically rigorous interval `[Maghrib(D), Maghrib(D+1))`. It features:
> • **The Waqt Clock**: A 24-hour continuous SI-minute grid measuring elapsed time from Maghrib, preserving the natural astronomical cadence.  
> • **Deterministic Solar Engine**: High-precision solar vector calculations accounting for elevation, atmospheric refraction, and ΔT.  
> • **Calendar Interoperability**: Pluggable Hijri calendar providers (including Umm al-Qura astronomical approximations).  
> • **Cross-Platform Ecosystem**: Pure Python core engine, offline embeddable library, FastAPI HTTP standard, and client SDKs.
>
