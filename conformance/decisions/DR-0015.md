## Decision

The project owner and technical developer/maintainer approve marking conformance contract 2.0.0 released with the completed changes recorded in this contract and its release manifest.

The approvers explicitly accept an exception to the governance requirement for independent-implementation review for this release. **No independent review was performed.** This is an owner-approved exception, not a representation that the review occurred. The exception applies only to this release and does not change the review requirements for future contract changes.

The independent Node implementation's exact-integer microsecond limit remains documented at approximately year 2255. Date-level vectors through 2300 do not demonstrate full-range instant conformance; this release makes no such claim. The waiver does not resolve or conceal that limitation.

No signature was requested or collected; this record documents the owners' approval and exception.

## Evidence

The project owner and technical developer/maintainer supplied approval on 2026-10-12 for the 2.0.0 release and confirmed that their review was not independent review. The approval was given by Dr. Salim Muhammed Redi and Mr. Hawas Muhaba Hussein.

## Consequences

- The contract may be marked released only after the release manifest and conformance reports have been regenerated and verified.
- The governance page must identify the owner approval and state plainly that independent review was not performed.
- The independent-review exception is not a precedent or standing waiver.
- Existing decision records that separately remain marked as awaiting ratification are not retroactively represented as individually ratified by this release decision.
