## Context
Several tabular patterns exist. Both implementations used the civil pattern, so no comparison could expose the choice. The year lengths were vectors but not prose.

## Decision
The provider `arithmetic-tabular` version `30-year-cycle-epoch-1948440` is defined as: civil epoch JDN 1948440 (Friday, proleptic Gregorian 0622-07-19), alternating 30/29-day months, and leap years at cycle positions 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 18, 21, 24, 26, 29 (`(11y+14) mod 30 < 11`). Another pattern is a different provider.

## Evidence
Computed in Sprint 16: the rule agrees with the provider's forward conversion on all 35,988 months of AH 1-2999 and round-trips 109,938 consecutive days (1900-2200). The 30 year-length vectors are promoted from `open` to `exact`.

## Consequences
The provider's identity is now pinned; any change to the pattern is a new provider id and a MAJOR contract change.

## Alternatives considered
Treat the pattern as unspecified: rejected, it leaves results irreproducible. Ship several patterns now: deferred, no demand recorded, see OQ-TAB-3.

## Configurability (religious / regional conventions)
The *choice of calendar* is religious and institutional and stays with the caller (`calendar_provider`). This ruling pins only what `arithmetic-tabular` means. It does not call the other patterns wrong, and does not call this pattern correct.

## Dissent and unresolved points
OQ-TAB-2: Gregorian dates before the epoch convert to AH year <= 0 and cannot be converted back; not decided. OQ-TAB-3: variants (other leap patterns, astronomical epoch) are not provided.

## Reopen when
A caller needs a different pattern: add it as a new provider through GOVERNANCE.md.

## Vectors affected
calendar-core: tab/month12-length/* (30 vectors) open -> exact.
