# DR-0001: Day label: civil date of the closing Maghrib instant

| | |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Accepted in Sprint 16 under the sprint brief. **Ratification by a named maintainer: PENDING** (see GOVERNANCE.md section 3) |
| **Contract** | 2.0.0 |
| **Date** | 2026-10-04 |
| **Questions** | OQ-DAY-1 (resolved), DIS-001 (fixed), consequence rule DAY-009 |
| **Requirements** | DAY-004, DAY-007, DAY-009 |

## Context
SEMANTICS section 3 and CALENDAR-INTEROP section 1 already said the label is the Gregorian *civil* date on which the closing Maghrib falls. The reference nevertheless took the label from the *solar-date key* handed to its solver, which differs from the civil date wherever the zone's offset is far from the longitude's mean-solar offset (Kiritimati, Apia, Chatham) or Maghrib falls after local midnight (Reykjavik in June). The reference also contradicted itself: `gregorian_date_to_waqt_day` already used the civil date.

## Decision
The label date is the civil date, in the location's IANA zone (or `timezone_override`), on which the closing Maghrib **instant** falls. Hijri date and weekday are those of that date. Events remain keyed by solar date (DR-0002). Because this makes labels non-injective near local midnight, DAY-009 states the consequences: labels never decrease; a civil date with no closing Maghrib is unresolved; a civil date with two resolves to the day containing its local noon.

## Evidence
Reproduced in the reference before the fix: Kiritimati 2026-09-19T22:00Z labelled 1448-04-06 / as-Sabt instead of 1448-04-07 / al-Ahad; Apia and Reykjavik likewise; Addis Ababa correct. After the fix all four agree with the civil rule. The independent implementation and the written text already agreed with the civil rule. Reykjavik 2026: no day closes on 15 June (23:58:53 on the 14th, then 00:00:09 on the 16th) and two close on 29 June (00:00:16 and 23:59:04); reference and independent implementation agree on both dates.

## Consequences
Behaviour change in `waqt-core` 0.4.0 for 4 of the 24 places in the Sprint 15 survey. The frozen Sprint 5 vector `kiritimati-date-line` (label 1448-04-06 / as-Sabt) was the defect and was corrected; vector set 1.1 -> 2.0 (MAJOR by `docs/VERSIONING.md`). New vectors `label/skip/*` and `label/repeat/*`.

## Alternatives considered
(B) Amend the text to say 'mean-solar date': rejected, it contradicts `gregorian_date_to_waqt_day`, makes a civil-date lookup fail at the date line, and would make the weekday disagree with every civil calendar. (C) A profile option choosing the labelling rule: not adopted now, no evidence of demand; see Configurability.

## Configurability (religious / regional conventions)
The Hijri day beginning at Maghrib is itself the convention and is not parameterised. The *calendar* used for the Hijri date stays fully configurable (`calendar_provider`). If a regional convention needs a different labelling of the weekday or date for a Maghrib-to-Maghrib day, it must be proposed as a new, named profile option with the default unchanged (GOVERNANCE.md section 6).

## Dissent and unresolved points
None recorded. The non-injective consequence is accepted as the price of agreeing with civil calendars; a reader who finds it unacceptable should file a proposal rather than a defect.

## Reopen when
A named regional convention requires a different label for after-midnight Maghribs, or a calendar authority publishes a contrary rule.

## Vectors affected
day-model: label/* (existing, now passing in the reference), label/skip/reykjavik/2026-06-15 and label/repeat/reykjavik/2026-06-29 (new); legacy-waqt: waqt/kiritimati-date-line label fields restored.
