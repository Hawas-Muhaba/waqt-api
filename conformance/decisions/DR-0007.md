## Context
The reference answered for 1650-2300 and failed obscurely outside; the independent implementation answered 1700-2300 with `OUT_OF_RANGE`. The chain origin was unstated. Test inputs (limb, refraction, elevation, algorithms) were unstated.

## Decision
Resolved: the supported range is 1700-01-01..2300-12-31 and the provider refuses with `OUT_OF_RANGE` outside it; the month chain is self-synchronising (measured evidence in CALENDARS.md). **Not resolved:** the conventions that decide whether the Moon sets after the Sun at Makkah. Those vectors stay `informative` and CAL-007 stays open.

## Evidence
Range guard verified with a stubbed ephemeris (the guard runs before any ephemeris call); the Umm al-Qura computation itself could not be executed in the Sprint 16 sandbox (PyEphem unavailable) and must be confirmed by CI. Sprint 15 evidence: 2 of 492 month starts differ, both evenings where the Moon sets 3.7 s and 6.8 s before the Sun.

## Consequences
Behaviour change for 1650-1699 (now `OUT_OF_RANGE`). Boundary vectors added as `informative`.

## Alternatives considered
Pick PyEphem's or the independent implementation's conventions as normative: rejected, that would present an institutional convention as a mathematical fact and manufacture a clean report.

## Configurability (religious / regional conventions)
This is the clearest religious/institutional case. The official table is produced by an authority; the written rule is an approximation. The right way to follow the authority exactly is a `declared` provider fed from the official table. No convention is hard-coded as 'the' answer.

## Dissent and unresolved points
OQ-UAQ-2 is deliberately left open. Two implementations that follow the text differ on 2 of 492 months; that difference is recorded, not removed.

## Reopen when
The authority publishes its computational conventions, or a calendar-source decision is taken by the project owners.

## Vectors affected
calendar-uaq: uaq/range/* promoted to exact (OUT_OF_RANGE); 1700-01-01 and 2300-12-31 boundary vectors added (informative).
